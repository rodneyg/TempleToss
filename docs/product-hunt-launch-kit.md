## Description

Tap Jump and Toss in Temple Toss, a free browser game for phone and desktop. Jump over obstacles, grab yellow orbs for higher jumps, and toss at bullseyes for points. Hold Jump to go higher, steer in midair, and try to beat the top score saved in your browser.
